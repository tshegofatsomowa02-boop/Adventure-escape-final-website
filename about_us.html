About Adventure Escape SA

Adventure Escape SA is focused on creating memorable outdoor experiences that bring people closer to nature while encouraging confidence, teamwork, and active living.

Our activities are designed around outdoor adventure, scenic landscapes, and practical enjoyment for people of different experience levels.

The company promotes a positive and supportive adventure culture, whether the goal is a single personal challenge, a family weekend, or a team-building event for a company.

We believe adventure should be exciting, accessible, and well planned.

Our Mission

Adventure Escape SA's mission is to give famailies and individuals the opportunity whilst on vacation to choose from a variety of exciting outdoor activities to book from at affordable prices.

Our Vission

At Adventure Escape SA we vision to become a leading South African outdoor activity booking company that inspires both local and tourists to explore the country's hidden gems!

Our Goal

To offer safe, enjoyable, and inspiring outdoor adventures that connect people with South Africa's natural beauty and active lifestyle opportunities.
